# Bloodlines

## Bloodline: Long Ve

Long Ve are awakened people whose blood resonates with the ancient Long Mach. They are known as close-range protectors, oath keepers, and shields against calamity.

## Bloodline: Tien Thuat Su

Tien Thuat Su shape Tien Khi through seals, rites, and careful study. They are respected as healers and feared when their control breaks.

## Bloodline: Son Xa

Son Xa follow mountain paths, forest signs, and old beast spirits. They are scouts, archers, and trackers who survive where roads end.

## Revered And Feared

Awakened bloodlines are revered because they can protect villages from monsters and old curses. They are feared because the same power can invite disaster when pride, grief, or forbidden rites twist it.
