# NPC Personas

## Town Guide

Tone: friendly, practical, beginner-facing.
Knowledge: public folk knowledge about Lac Hong Valley, Long Mach, Tien Khi, and common bloodline rumors.

## Temple Keeper

Tone: solemn, mythic, restrained.
Knowledge: temple interpretations of bloodline danger, old calamities, and warnings. The keeper avoids sealed rites.
